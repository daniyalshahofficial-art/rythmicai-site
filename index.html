Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know.

A 3D animated website uses WebGL, Three.js, and scroll-triggered animations to create immersive, interactive digital experiences. Unlike traditional static websites, 3D animated sites respond to user input with smooth motion, parallax effects, and real-time 3D rendering — turning a standard web page into a memorable brand experience that increases engagement and conversion rates.

Agentic AI automations are autonomous AI systems that go beyond simple chatbots. They use large language models and custom tool integrations to independently execute multi-step workflows — such as qualifying leads, following up with prospects, processing data, and coordinating across multiple business systems — without requiring human intervention for each step.

RythmicAI works with founders, agencies, growth teams, DTC brands, SaaS companies, and creative studios who want a digital presence that stands out. Our clients typically need either a premium 3D animated website that differentiates their brand or AI-powered automation systems that eliminate manual processes and scale operations.

At RythmicAI, most 3D animated websites are designed, built, and deployed within a single day. Our founder works directly with clients from discovery through launch, leveraging deep expertise in WebGL, Three.js, and GSAP to move from concept to live site in hours rather than weeks. For more complex multi-page experiences with custom 3D models, interactive configurators, or heavy animation choreography, delivery typically extends to 2-3 days. Every project includes same-day deployment to a production environment with SSL, CDN, and performance optimization out of the box.

Simple agentic AI automations — such as lead qualification bots, automated reporting workflows, or AI-powered email responders — are typically built and deployed within a single day at RythmicAI. For more complex multi-agent systems involving CRM integrations, databases, custom tool chains, and multi-step decision logic, delivery takes 1-2 weeks. Enterprise-grade systems with extensive testing requirements, edge case handling, compliance checks, and phased rollouts can take 2-4 weeks. Testing timelines scale with system complexity: simple agents can be validated in hours, while multi-agent workflows with production data require iterative testing cycles to ensure reliability, accuracy, and safe failure handling.
